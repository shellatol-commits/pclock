# A Lock for Your PC

PC Lock adds an alarm to help protect your computer against theft.

## Step-by-Step Setup

### 1. Set Up Your Master Password

Configure the master password for your user account. This is the password associated with your account that you use to unlock your PC.

### 2. Connect to Your Wi-Fi Network

Connect your PC to the Wi-Fi network you want to designate as a favorite network.

### 3. Add the Network to PC Lock

Open the PC Lock window and enter the Wi-Fi password in the text field. Click **"Set as a fav network"** to register the network. The window will close once the network has been added.

### 4. Add More Favorite Networks (Optional)

If you want to register additional networks, connect to each desired Wi-Fi network, reopen the PC Lock window, and repeat Step 3.

### 5. Understand How the Alarm Works

If someone steals your computer and attempts to unlock it by entering your account password, an alarm countdown will appear. Any action performed through the alarm panel requires a code.

### 6. Know What to Do if the Alarm Is Triggered

If your computer is stolen and the alarm activates, read the **Rescue Information** section below for instructions on how to respond and use the available rescue methods.
